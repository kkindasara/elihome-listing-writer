## 3. Description
The Elihome Classic Silicone Spatula Turner is one utensil for the cook who wants a single turner from breakfast to dinner. In the morning, it flips pancakes and eggs and turns crepes. In the evening, it turns a fish fillet, stirs sautéing vegetables and flips burgers, on the stovetop or on the grill. It is also recommended for baking, so the same turner works for sweet recipes as well as savory ones.

Because non-stick cookware is one of its recommended uses, it fits a kitchen built around coated frying pans. Its Small size and rectangular shape suit a frying pan on the stove, and it goes back in the utensil drawer with the rest of your tools when the cooking is done.

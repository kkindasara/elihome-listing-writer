## 7. To check before publishing
- **Size and weight of each board (MISSING).** "Item dimensions 14 x 10 x 0.7 in" and "2 pounds" do not say whether they describe one board or the set. Amazon's set guidance asks for the size of each piece (B13), and "how big is each board?" is the first size question a shopper or Alexa for Shopping will ask (AI2). The bullets use the figures exactly as the specs give them; once confirmed, write "each board 14 x 10 in" in bullets 1 and 5.
- **Max temperature (MISSING).** The specs say "not given". Pizza is a listed use, so shoppers will ask about hot food. A heat limit needs a test report (C2); until then no heat claim appears anywhere.
- **Country of origin (MISSING).** It has its own field (A6) and AI assistants need it (AI5). Any "Made in" wording must meet C6.
- **Warranty terms (MISSING).** The specs say only "Limited (no description)", so the warranty is kept out of the bullets. With a length and what it covers, it can go in the last bullet (X5); never a refund promise (B11, C10).
- **Material composition and food-contact safety (MISSING).** "Wood Fiber" is the only material fact. Food safe, food grade and BPA-free were left out of all customer text and search terms: composition and free-from claims need a lab test report or certification, and supplier statements are not enough (C2, C5, K8).
- **"Non-Porous" left out.** It is in "Additional features" with no proof. It is a material and performance claim that needs a lab test (C1, C2), and it is often read as a hygiene claim, which must never drift into antibacterial wording (C8, C12).
- **"Non-slip" left out.** The specs name the feet "Non Slip Feet" but give no test. Non-slip is a performance claim (B8, C1), so the feet are described by color only. If the packaging states it, or there is a slip test, it can return to bullet 1 and the search terms (B8). The feet material is also MISSING.
- **Feet and reversible design.** Confirm how the feet work when a board is turned over, and which side or sides have the groove. Shoppers will ask how a board with feet can be used on both sides.
- **Color split.** "Natural and Black" does not say which board is which color, or whether both are two-toned. This is a common shopper question.
- **Variation family.** The Peanuts 2-pack sits in one family with three Pickleball designs at a different price ($55.24 vs $67.99). Confirm they belong under the same parent. This draft covers only the Double Double 2 Pack Set (T12).
- **UPC / GTIN.** Confirm that 850081613039 is the GTIN of the 2 Pack Set and that Elihome owns it (A7). The Pickleball variants' GTINs are MISSING, and AI5 asks for a GTIN on every variant, the same on Amazon, Walmart and Wayfair.
- **Peanuts name in bullets.** If the Peanuts license requires ™ or ® after the name, note that those symbols are prohibited in bullets (B9); check the licensor's rules for how the name may appear without them.
- **Item type keyword.** "Cutting Boards" comes from the category path; confirm the exact spelling in the Browse Tree Guide (A5).
- **Price consistency.** The price ($67.99, typical $79.99) is kept out of customer text (T11, D2). Alexa for Shopping shows price history (AI4) and AI5 asks for the same price across channels, so check Walmart and Wayfair.

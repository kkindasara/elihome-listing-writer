## Product description

Meet the Elihome Classic Silicone Spatula Turner, a simple, dependable tool for the jobs you do every day at the stove.

Its rectangular silicone head is made for non-stick cookware, so you can slide under a fried egg, a stack of pancakes or a thin crepe and turn it over cleanly. The same turner handles burgers, fish fillets, sautéed vegetables, grilling and baking, which makes it an easy one-tool answer for most of your cooking.

The compact size is part of the design. At 11.5 inches long and 2.7 inches wide, it reaches comfortably into skillets and frying pans without feeling oversized, and it weighs only 3.2 ounces. The silicone handle keeps your grip soft and comfortable from the first pancake to the last.

Made from silicone and stainless steel, it comes in four colors (Dark Gray, Teal, Sage and Red), so you can pick the one that suits your kitchen or keep a few on hand.

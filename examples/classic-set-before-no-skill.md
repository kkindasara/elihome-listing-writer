## Product description

Meet the Elihome Classic Series 4-in-1 Cutting Board Set: four wood fiber boards in four sizes, so every job in the kitchen has a board that fits it.

The set includes a Small board (10" x 7") for quick tasks like garlic, herbs and citrus; a Medium board (13" x 10") for everyday vegetable and fruit prep; a Large board (16" x 12") for meat, fish and chicken; and an Extra Large board (19" x 14") for big jobs like carving a roast, shaping pizza dough or setting out a BBQ spread.

Every board is made of wood fiber with a non-porous surface that does not soak up liquids, plus a built-in groove to help contain juices. The boards are scratch resistant and heat resistant, and cleanup is simple: they are dishwasher safe, so there is no oiling or special care routine to keep up with.

With a natural finish and a solid rectangular design, the boards look just as good on the table as they do on the counter. Use them to serve bread, cheese and fruit, then send them through the dishwasher when the party is over.

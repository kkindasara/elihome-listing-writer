## 3. Description
The Elihome Classic Series set puts four wood fiber cutting boards of different sizes in one kitchen, so each job gets a board that fits it.

Slice an apple or a wedge of cheese on the small board. Move to the medium or large board for a loaf of bread or a pizza. When you cut meat, fish or chicken, or carve food from the barbecue, reach for a larger board and use the built-in groove for the juices.

With four boards out at once, one person can trim raw chicken while another cuts bread or fruit on a separate board. The boards share the same rectangular shape and natural color, so they look like one set whether a single board is out or all four.

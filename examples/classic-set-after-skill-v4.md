## 3. Description
This Elihome Classic Series set puts four cutting board sizes in one place, so the board can match the task in front of you. Reach for the small board when you are slicing an apple or a few pieces of cheese for a snack, the medium board for a loaf of bread, and the large or extra-large board when the job is bigger, such as cutting up chicken for dinner, preparing fish, slicing a pizza or getting meat ready for a barbecue.

Because the boards come in graduated sizes, you can match the board to the amount of food rather than clearing counter space for a big board every time. The set suits a single cook making a weeknight meal as well as a household preparing food for guests, and all four boards share the same wood fiber material and natural color.
